    Open and upload iPhone photos anywhere

    iPhones save photos as HEIC files, which keeps them small but causes trouble when a job portal, exam form or office computer only accepts JPG. Drop your HEIC photos here and they are turned into normal JPG images that open on every phone, PC and website. You can convert a whole batch and download them together as a ZIP.

    Fast, private and free

    The conversion happens on your own device, so your personal photos are never sent to a server. Large photos can take a few seconds each on older phones. If a form also has a size limit, run the JPG through the Resize Image to KB tool afterwards to bring it under 50 KB, 100 KB or whatever the form asks for.
