Pull out just the pages you need

Only need a few pages of a long report, a single chapter or one signed form? Add the PDF, type the pages you want, such as 1-3, 5, 8-10, and click Split PDF. You get a new PDF with only those pages, in the order you typed them.

Split a PDF into single pages, privately

Choose the second option to turn every page into its own PDF. The files arrive together in one ZIP download. Everything runs in your browser, so contracts, bank statements and other private documents never leave your device.
