Fix sideways and upside-down pages

Scanned documents and phone scans often come out sideways. Add your PDF, pick the direction and click Rotate PDF. You can turn every page at once, or only the pages that are wrong, such as 2, 4-6.

Saved for good, kept private

The new rotation is stored in the file itself, so the PDF opens the right way in any reader, on any device, and prints correctly. The work happens in your browser, so your document is never uploaded.
