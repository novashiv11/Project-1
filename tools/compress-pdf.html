Shrink a PDF for email and upload forms

Many email services and online application forms reject files above a few megabytes. Choose your PDF, pick a compression level and click Compress PDF. You will see the size before and after, so you know straight away whether it fits the limit. Low compression keeps pages sharp, high compression gives the smallest file.

Be aware of how it works: every page is redrawn as a compressed JPEG image. That makes scanned documents and photo-heavy PDFs much smaller, but the text in the result can no longer be selected, searched or copied, and stronger levels look softer. PDFs that are mostly plain text are often already small; if compressing would make yours bigger, the tool tells you and offers the original instead.

Your documents stay on your device

The compression runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, so it is safe for contracts, payslips and ID scans. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on the number of files.
