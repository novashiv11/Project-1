QR codes that never expire

Every code made here is a static QR code: the link, Wi-Fi password or payment details are stored inside the pattern itself. It will keep working for as long as the printed code exists, with no account, no scan limits and no monthly fee. Many other QR sites sell "dynamic" codes that send each scan through their own server first, and those codes quietly stop working when a trial ends or a subscription lapses. Everything here is generated in your browser, so your Wi-Fi password and UPI ID are never sent anywhere.

Tips for codes that scan every time

Keep a dark colour on a light background and leave the white border around the code, because phones need contrast and a quiet zone to find it. For printed codes, aim for at least 2 to 3 cm wide and download the SVG so it stays sharp. Choose a higher error correction level if the code may get scratched or dirty, and always test it with your own phone before printing hundreds of copies.
