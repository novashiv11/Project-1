Number your PDF pages in seconds

Page numbers make reports, theses, manuals and printed handouts much easier to follow. Add your PDF, choose where the numbers go and how they look, for example "Page 3 of 12" at the bottom center, and click Add page numbers.

Flexible and private

Start from any number, skip a cover page and pick a font size that suits the document. Numbering happens in your browser, so the PDF never leaves your device and there is no watermark or sign-up.
