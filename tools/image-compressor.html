    Make images smaller without uploading them

    Many websites, job portals and government forms only accept photos under a certain size, and email attachments have limits too. This tool shrinks your images right in your browser: choose a quality level and a maximum width, then download the smaller files. Because nothing is uploaded, it is fast and completely private, even for personal documents.

    Tips for the best result

    For photos, a quality of 70–80% usually looks identical to the original at a fraction of the size. Reducing the width has the biggest effect: a phone photo is often 4000 pixels wide, but 1200–1600 pixels is plenty for screens and websites.
