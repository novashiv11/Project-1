Make a photo fit the KB limit on any form

Job applications, exam registrations and government portals often say things like "photo under 50 KB" or "signature between 10 and 20 KB". Instead of guessing with a quality slider, type the limit (or tap 20, 50, 100, 200 or 500 KB) and this tool finds the best quality that still fits. If quality alone is not enough, it gently reduces the picture size too. The final size in KB and pixels is shown before you download, so you know it will be accepted.

Exact pixel sizes for passport photos and signatures

Some forms also ask for exact dimensions, such as 200 × 230 pixels for a photo or 140 × 60 pixels for a signature. Enter the width and height and the image is resized to exactly that before it is compressed. Crop your photo first if it needs a different shape, so the face is not stretched. Everything runs in your browser, so your documents stay private.
