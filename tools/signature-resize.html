Resize your signature for exam and job applications

Online application forms for government exams, bank jobs, university admissions and recruitment portals usually ask for a scanned signature in a fixed pixel size and under a strict file size, such as 140 × 60 pixels and less than 20 KB. You do not need a scanner: sign on white paper with a dark pen, take a photo with your phone, and this tool crops it, removes the grey background, resizes it to the exact dimensions and squeezes the file under the limit. It all runs in your browser, so your signature is never uploaded. Requirements differ between forms and change over time, so always follow the official notification.

Tips for a clear, accepted signature

Use a black or dark blue pen and sign a little larger than usual so the strokes stay visible after shrinking. Photograph the paper flat, in daylight, without your shadow falling across it. If thin parts of the signature fade after cleaning, move the threshold slider to the right; if specks or shadows remain, move it to the left. Check the preview before downloading: the signature should be readable and fill most of the box.
