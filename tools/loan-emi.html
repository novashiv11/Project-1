How to use the loan EMI calculator

Enter the amount you want to borrow, the yearly interest rate from your lender and the loan length. You get the fixed monthly payment, the total interest over the life of the loan, and a year-by-year schedule showing how much of each year's payments went to interest versus the loan itself.

It works for home loans, mortgages, car loans, personal loans and student loans with a fixed interest rate.

Why early payments are mostly interest

Interest is charged on the remaining balance, which is highest at the start. That is why the first years of a long loan are mostly interest, and why paying extra early has the biggest impact.
