Tips for a photo that gets accepted

Stand about a metre in front of a plain white or light-coloured wall, face the camera straight on with a neutral expression and both eyes open, and make sure there are no shadows on your face or behind you. Remove glasses if the rules for your country require it, and avoid hats or anything covering your face unless it is worn for religious or medical reasons. When printing the sheet, turn off any "fit to page" or "scale" option so each photo comes out at its true size.
