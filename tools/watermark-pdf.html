Mark documents as draft or confidential

A watermark tells readers at a glance that a document is a draft, confidential, a sample or a copy. Type your text, choose diagonal or straight across, then set the size, color and how see-through it should be. Every page gets the same watermark.

No upload, no sign-up

Documents that need a watermark are often the ones you least want to upload. This tool adds the watermark in your browser, so the file stays on your device. Keep the opacity low, around 20 to 30 percent, so the page underneath stays easy to read.
