Crop photos to the right shape for forms and profiles

Application forms often want a photo that shows just your face and shoulders, and social sites want square profile pictures. Add your image, choose a shape such as 1:1 for square or 3:4 for passport-style photos, then drag the box into place and resize it from any corner. The size of the crop in pixels is shown as you go, and it works with a finger on a phone as well as with a mouse.

Full quality, nothing uploaded

The cropped picture keeps the original resolution, so it stays sharp. Because cropping happens inside your browser, your photo is never sent anywhere. If the form also has a size limit in KB or needs exact pixels, open the Resize Image to KB tool next and enter the numbers it asks for.
