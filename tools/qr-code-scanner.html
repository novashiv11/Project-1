Read a QR code without opening it

Your phone camera usually jumps straight to whatever a QR code points to. This scanner shows you the full content first: the exact web address, Wi-Fi details, payment link or contact card. Upload a photo or screenshot, paste an image, or use your camera. Everything is decoded on your own device, so the image never leaves it. It is handy for codes in emails, PDFs and screenshots that you cannot point a camera at.

How to spot a suspicious QR code

QR scams are increasingly common on parking meters, restaurant tables, delivery notes and messages. Look closely at the link: check the spelling of the domain, be careful with shortened links, and be wary of codes stuck on top of other codes. For UPI codes, remember that you never need to scan a code or enter your PIN to receive money. If anything looks off, type the website address yourself instead.
