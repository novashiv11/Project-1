How this compound interest calculator works

Enter what you start with, how much you add each month, the expected yearly return and how long you will invest. The calculator compounds the interest at the frequency you choose and adds your contribution at the end of every month. The table shows how much of your balance came from your own deposits and how much came from interest.

Why starting early matters

Because interest also earns interest, time is the biggest factor. Someone who invests $200 a month from age 25 to 35 and then stops can end up with more at 65 than someone who invests $200 a month from 35 to 65, at the same return. Try changing the years above to see the effect.
