LifeOS privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026

The short version

Everything you type, scan or log stays on your phone. LifeOS has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics.

What is stored, and where

  • Your items, food log and settings are saved in your browser's storage on this device only.
  • Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes them. Use All → Back up now to keep a copy; the backup file is yours and goes wherever you choose to save or share it. Your Claude API key is never included in backups.

When data leaves your phone

  • Barcode scans: the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. Nothing else is sent.
  • Jarvis chat (optional): only if you add your own Claude API key. Then your message, a list of your open items and any photo you choose to send go to Anthropic's API to produce a reply, under Anthropic's privacy policy. Your key is stored only on this phone.
  • Voice input: uses your phone's built-in speech recognition (Google on Android), which may process audio on their servers.
  • Links you open (for example ncts.ie or irishimmigration.ie) are ordinary websites with their own policies.

What LifeOS never does

  • It never asks for or stores passport, IRP, PPSN or bank numbers. Jarvis is instructed to save dates only.
  • It never sells or shares data. There is no one to share it with: we don't have it.
