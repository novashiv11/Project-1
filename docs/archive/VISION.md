# Vision

**One place to throw your life at.** Zero-friction capture → automatic sorting → timely nudges → a journal of growth.

## Who
- **Me** — ideas, goals, mood, habits, documents, money, health.
- **Dog** — vet, vaccines, food, meds, grooming, walks, weight.
- **Home** — rent/mortgage, repairs, appliances & warranties, bills, chores.
- **Logistics** — packages, passport/visa/licence expiry, insurance renewals, calendar.

## Principles
1. **Capture in < 3 seconds.** One line of text (later: voice, share-sheet, Telegram/WhatsApp bot, email forward).
2. **Never ask for structure.** The system infers domain, date, entities.
3. **Surface, don't store.** Value = the right reminder at the right time ("passport expires in 90 days — renew now").
4. **Local-first & private.** Your data is yours; cloud sync is optional and encrypted.
5. **Grows with you.** Weekly review + journal shows patterns (mood, habits, dog health, spend).

## North-star metric
Items captured per week that later produced a useful nudge or answer.
